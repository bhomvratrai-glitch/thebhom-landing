What is TheBhom?
TheBhom is India's free digital content platform. It gives you access to over 1 crore ebooks, 6,34,000+ magazines, 150+ 4K AMOLED wallpapers, 43+ design templates, and 44+ greeting cards — all completely free with no sign-up or subscription required.
Is TheBhom completely free to use?
Yes, TheBhom is 100% free. There are no hidden charges, no premium tiers, and no registration required for any downloads. Every ebook, magazine, wallpaper, template, and card on the platform is available at zero cost.
What kind of content does TheBhom offer?
TheBhom offers five main categories: (1) free public domain and educational ebooks including NCERT books and Hindi literature, (2) curated digital periodicals, educational journals, and public domain archives, (3) 150+ 4K AMOLED dark wallpapers with zero watermarks, (4) 43+ professional design templates for Instagram and YouTube, and (5) 44+ HD greeting cards for all occasions.
Can I use TheBhom to download NCERT books?
Yes. TheBhom's ebooks section includes all NCERT textbooks from Class 1 to Class 12, available as free PDF downloads. They are sourced from official open-access government repositories and are completely legal to download.
Is TheBhom available in Hindi?
Yes, TheBhom supports Hindi content throughout the platform. The ebooks library includes a dedicated Hindi Sahitya (Hindi literature) section with works by authors like Premchand and Harivansh Rai Bachchan. The platform interface also supports both Hindi and English.
