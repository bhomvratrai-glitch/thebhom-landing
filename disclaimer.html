4. Hardware Benchmarks, Thermal Testing & Performance Variance

Throughout our 105+ in-depth technical guides, processor comparisons, and smartphone/laptop analyses, we frequently cite benchmark scores (including AnTuTu v10, Geekbench 6, 3DMark Wild Life Extreme, PCMark, and Cinebench R23), thermal throttling percentages, and battery life estimates.

⚠️ Real-World Hardware Variance Notice

Benchmark scores and battery endurance figures published on TheBhom represent testing performed under controlled laboratory conditions or verified reference engineering benchmarks. Actual real-world performance will naturally vary depending on:

  • Ambient room temperature and seasonal climatic conditions (e.g., thermal throttling in non-air-conditioned tropical environments).
  • OEM software builds, manufacturer skin overhead (e.g., One UI, HyperOS, ColorOS, OxygenOS), and background system daemons.
  • Silicon lottery variances across identical fabrication node batches.
  • Display brightness levels, network band strength (5G Sub-6 vs. 4G LTE vs. Wi-Fi 6E/7), and user application workloads.

Scores are provided to illustrate comparative relative performance tiers across silicon generations rather than absolute guarantees of frame rates or rendering speeds on any specific retail unit.
