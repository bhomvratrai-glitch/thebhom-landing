How to Reduce Image Size

Make your images smaller for faster loading, easier sharing, and less storage.

Method 1: Compress

Use our Image Compressor to reduce file size while keeping the same dimensions. Best for web uploads and email.

Method 2: Resize

Use our Image Resizer to change dimensions. A 4000x3000 photo resized to 1920x1440 is already much smaller.

Method 3: Convert Format

Convert PNG to JPG for photos (no transparency needed) or to WEBP for best web compression using our converter.

FAQ

What is the best image format for web?

WEBP offers the best compression for web. JPG is great for photos, PNG for graphics with transparency.

How much can I reduce an image?

Typically 50-80% with our balanced compression. The exact reduction depends on the image content.

Will compression make my image blurry?

Our balanced mode maintains excellent visual quality. Only maximum compression may show slight differences on very close inspection.
