How to Merge PDF Files Online Free (Step-by-Step)

Need to combine multiple PDFs into one? Here\'s the fastest way to do it, for free, on any device.

September 11, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer: Go to ImgPDF Merge Tool, upload your PDFs, drag to reorder, click merge. Done.

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Open the Merge Tool: Go to imgpdf.thebhom.in/pdf/merge
  2. Upload Files: Drag and drop multiple PDFs, or click to browse. Upload up to 10 files at once.
  3. Reorder (Optional): Drag files to change their order. The first file becomes the first pages of the merged document.
  4. Click "Merge PDF": Processing takes 2-5 seconds depending on file sizes.
  5. Download: Your merged PDF downloads automatically. You\'ll see the file size and how long it took.

Common Use Cases

  • Combining reports: Merge monthly reports into a quarterly summary
  • Job applications: Combine resume, cover letter, and certificates into one PDF
  • Legal documents: Merge contracts, amendments, and appendices
  • School projects: Combine different sections written by team members
  • Invoice batches: Merge multiple invoices for accounting

Tips for Best Results

  • Name your files in order (01-intro.pdf, 02-body.pdf) before uploading
  • Check page orientation: mix of landscape and portrait pages work fine
  • For very large files (100MB+), compress each PDF first using the Compress PDF tool
  • The merged file preserves all formatting, links, and bookmarks from the original PDFs
