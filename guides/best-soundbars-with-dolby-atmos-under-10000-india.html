1. HDMI ARC / eARC vs Optical vs Bluetooth: Use the Right Cable

Never connect a TV soundbar over Bluetooth if your TV sits 2 feet away—Bluetooth compresses audio and causes lip-sync drift. Plug an HDMI High-Speed cable into your TV's HDMI ARC/eARC port. One TV remote will automatically power on the soundbar and control its volume (CEC) while passing uncompressed PCM/Dolby Digital+.

2. 3 Things That Matter Most Under ₹10,000

  • Dedicated Center Channel (3.1) or Clear Voice EQ: Prevents background explosions from drowning out whispered movie dialogues on Prime Video and Hotstar.
  • Subwoofer Driver Size: Look for at least a 5.25-inch or 6.5-inch subwoofer (60W+ RMS) rather than inflated '500W PMPO' peak numbers.
  • Wall-Mount & IR Pass-Through Height: Check soundbar height (< 65mm) so it doesn't block your Smart TV's bottom IR remote sensor on a TV cabinet.

3. Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Do ₹8,000 'Dolby Atmos' soundbars have real overhead speakers?
Almost never—at ₹8k–₹10k, Atmos decoding uses DSP psychoacoustic widening on front-firing drivers. True physical up-firing Atmos bars typically start around ₹18,000–₹25,000+.
❓ What does RMS wattage mean vs PMPO?
Always compare RMS (Root Mean Square) continuous wattage (e.g., 120W–160W RMS), never peak marketing PMPO numbers.
❓ Wired subwoofer vs wireless subwoofer under ₹10k?
A wireless subwoofer lets you place the bass box next to your sofa without running cables across the living room, though wired subwoofers never suffer wireless pairing interference.
❓ Why is dialogue volume so low on 5.1 movies when using a 2.1 soundbar?
Set your TV audio output to 'PCM' or turn on the soundbar's 'News / Voice / Night' EQ mode so the center dialogue track isn't buried.
