1. Jio True 5G (SA) vs Airtel 5G Plus (NSA): Why Band Support Differs

Jio operates a Standalone (SA) 5G core built around n78 (3500 MHz) for speed and n28 (700 MHz) for indoor basement/bedroom reach. If you buy an imported or older budget phone missing n28, Jio 5G will frequently drop inside buildings.

Airtel uses Non-Standalone (NSA) 5G anchored onto 4G LTE bands (like Band 1, 3, 8, 40) combined with n78. Therefore, your phone needs strong 4G-to-5G EN-DC combo support as well as VoNR (Voice over 5G New Radio) for crisp Jio 5G calls without dropping back to 4G.

2. Minimum 5G Band Checklist Before Buying Any Phone in India

  • Must include n28 and n78 at minimum.
  • Prefer phones with 9+ Indian 5G bands (n1, n3, n5, n8, n28, n40, n41, n77, n78) and 5G Carrier Aggregation (2CA/3CA).
  • Ensure Dual 5G SIM Standby and VoNR toggle are enabled in software.

3. Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How many 5G bands do I actually need in India?
You don't need 15 global bands—having n28, n78, n77, n1, n3, n5, n8, n40, and n41 covers virtually every Indian circle across Jio, Airtel, and Vi.
❓ Why does my 5G icon show full bars but internet feels slow?
On Airtel NSA, the 5G icon sometimes lights up because the tower supports 5G anchor even when traffic is routed over congested 4G. Switching to '5G Only' or checking n78 signal strength reveals true 5G lock.
❓ Should I buy an imported US/China phone for use in India?
Be careful: Chinese variants often lack Jio's n28 (700MHz) or Band 40/41 combos, and US models can lack Indian VoNR/carrier configs.
❓ Does 5G drain more battery than 4G?
In weak indoor coverage (1–2 bars), yes—especially on NSA where the modem maintains simultaneous 4G and 5G connections. Enabling 'Smart 5G' auto-switches to 4G when idle.
