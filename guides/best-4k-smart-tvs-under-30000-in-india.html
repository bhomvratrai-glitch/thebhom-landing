1. VA vs IPS/ADS Panels: Match the TV to Your Living Room

If your family watches IPL cricket or daily soaps from side sofas with room lights on, an IPS / ADS panel keeps colors accurate up to 178° off-angle. If you watch Netflix and movies in a dimly lit bedroom sitting straight in front, a VA panel delivers 3–4x deeper blacks without washed-out grey letterbox bars.

2. Why Smart TV RAM Matters—or Just Add a ₹3,500 Streaming Stick Later

Budget ₹22k–₹30k TVs often pack slow quad-core A53/A55 chips and 2GB RAM. Keep unnecessary apps uninstalled, switch off home-screen video autoplay, or plug an Apple TV / Fire TV Stick 4K into HDMI later if the UI slows down after 3 years—buy the TV for its panel quality and warranty, not its smart OS!

3. Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Is a ₹26,000 'QLED' TV much better than a regular 4K LED TV?
Quantum-dot film boosts color volume (greens and reds pop nicely in animation/sports), though without local dimming zones contrast and HDR peak brightness remain similar to standard Direct-LED TVs.
❓ 43-inch vs 50/55-inch under ₹30,000?
If your sofa is 7–9 feet away, stretch to a 50-inch model during festive sales—screen size creates more cinematic immersion than minor picture-processing tweaks.
❓ Why does cricket motion look blurry on cheap 4K TVs?
Turn off excessive 'Noise Reduction' and set MEMC / Motion Smoothing to 'Low' (or Off for 24fps films to avoid the soap-opera effect).
❓ Is an extended warranty worth buying on budget Smart TVs in India?
Yes—out-of-warranty 43"–50" display panel replacements cost 70–80% of a new TV, so a 2-year or 3-year brand extended warranty offers genuine peace of mind.
