1. Rule #1: Always Inspect the 'Sold By' Seller Name First

Even when a product page looks official (like Apple, SanDisk, or Minimalist), third-party marketplace sellers can attach their cheaper offer onto the same Buy Box. Before clicking Buy Now, verify the seller is an authorized distributor—such as Clicktech Retail, Cocoblu Retail, RK World Infocom, SuperComNet, or RetailNet—or the brand's own verified storefront.

2. 3 Instant Hardware & Legal Checks After Delivery

  • Official BIS Care App Check: Every power adapter, power bank, smartwatch, and earbud imported or sold legally in India carries an R-XXXXXXXX registration number. Enter it in the free BIS Care app to verify the brand and factory match.
  • Official Companion App Handshake: Counterfeit TWS earbuds and headphones look identical outside, but cheap JL/Bluetrum clone chips cannot connect to Sony Sound Connect, JBL Headphones, or Samsung Wearable apps.
  • Fake Capacity Test for Flash Drives: Run ValiDrive or H2testw on Windows immediately on any newly bought microSD card or pendrive before storing family photos on it.

3. Frequently Asked Questions

❓ If a listing says 'Amazon Fulfilled' or 'Flipkart Assured', can it still be fake?
Yes—rarely, 'commingled' or returned stock swapped by a previous dishonest buyer can slip into fulfillment bins. Always record an unboxing video and inspect seals.
❓ Why are 'Apple 20W Adapters' sold for ₹599 on marketplaces?
Original Apple 20W USB-C adapters never sell for ₹599—anything drastically below ₹1,500–₹1,900 from unverified sellers is almost certainly counterfeit and lacks safe surge isolation.
❓ What should I do if a product fails brand serial-number verification?
Do not open or use it aggressively—photograph the serial sticker, raise a return under 'Counterfeit / Fake Product' immediately, and escalate to National Consumer Helpline (1915) if rejected.
❓ Are 'Import / Open-Box Unboxed' deals safe?
Only from authorized brand-refurbished partners; random seller 'imported unboxed' Apple/Samsung accessories are overwhelmingly high-grade clones.
