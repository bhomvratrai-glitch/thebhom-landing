1. The 6-Step Inspection Within 24 Hours of Delivery

  1. Sanchar Saathi / CEIR IMEI Check: Verify both IMEI 1 & IMEI 2 on the government Sanchar Saathi portal to confirm the phone is never blacklisted or stolen.
  2. iPhone 'Parts & Service History': Go to Settings → General → About. Check if Display or Battery shows 'Unknown Part' (non-genuine aftermarket replacement) and ensure Battery Maximum Capacity is ≥ 85%.
  3. True Tone & Face ID / Fingerprint Test: Missing True Tone on iPhones usually signals a cheap aftermarket LCD/OLED screen swap.
  4. Camera OIS & Dust Check: Shoot a plain white wall at 1x, 0.5x, and 2x/3x to catch lens dust spots or shaky focus motors.
  5. SIM Call + Speaker + Mic Loopback: Make a 5-minute voice call and record a voice note to check earpiece mesh and noise-cancelling mics.

2. Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Are refurbished phones waterproof (IP67/IP68)?
Never assume water resistance on a refurbished phone—if it was opened for a battery or screen swap, factory adhesive seals are rarely restored to IP68 standard.
❓ Do Amazon Renewed or Cashify phones come with original chargers?
Usually they include a generic compatible USB cable/adapter rather than the original retail box brick—check the listing notes carefully.
❓ Is buying a refurbished iPhone better than a new ₹25,000 Android phone?
For video recording and social media apps, a clean renewed iPhone 13/14 shoots cleaner 4K HDR video; for 120Hz OLED, fast 67W charging, and 2-day battery, a new ₹25,000 Android wins.
❓ Should I buy offline from Cashify stores or online?
Visiting a physical Cashify store lets you check the exact screen, battery health %, and frame condition in your hand before paying.
