1. Why 'Instant Bank Sale Discounts' Beat Cashback Cards During BBD/GIF

Many shoppers don't realize that Amazon Pay ICICI and co-branded cashback cards are often excluded from Amazon Great Indian Festival and Flipkart Big Billion Days 10% instant discount banners (which require core ICICI, SBI, HDFC, or Axis credit cards). Keeping one Lifetime-Free core bank card alongside a 5% online cashback card covers both daily shopping and mega-sales.

2. 4 Credit Card Traps That Wipe Out Your Cashback

  • No-Cost EMI Interest + GST + ₹199–₹299 Processing Fee: Banks still charge 18% GST on the interest component plus processing fees, and most cards award zero cashback on EMI conversions.
  • Merchant Category Code (MCC) Surprise: Buying Amazon Pay Gift Cards via third-party portals often triggers Wallet/Gift MCCs that earn 0% cashback.
  • 1% Utility Surcharge Over ₹15,000–₹50,000: Several banks now add a 1% + GST surcharge on high monthly utility or education payments.
  • Paying Only 'Minimum Amount Due': Attracts 36%–44% APR interest immediately on the full balance—always enable Auto-Pay for Total Amount Due.

3. Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Does checking my credit card eligibility hurt my CIBIL score?
Checking pre-approved offers inside your bank app is a soft check (no impact), whereas submitting formal applications triggers hard inquiries—avoid applying for 3+ cards in the same month.
❓ Is Lifetime Free (LTF) better than a ₹999 annual-fee card?
Only pay an annual fee if your guaranteed yearly cashback exceeds the fee by at least 4–5x (or if your annual spend easily hits the fee-waiver threshold, e.g., ₹2 lakh/year).
❓ Can I get cashback on UPI payments via RuPay Credit Cards?
Yes, RuPay credit cards linked to BHIM/GPay/PhonePe earn rewards on merchant QR scans above ₹100–₹200, though P2P transfers to friends are blocked.
❓ Should I close an old Lifetime-Free credit card I rarely use?
Keep your oldest LTF card open with 1 small recurring bill on autopay—closing old accounts shrinks average credit history age and can dip your CIBIL score.
