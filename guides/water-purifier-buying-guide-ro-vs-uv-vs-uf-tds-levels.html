1. Step 1: Spend ₹250 on a Digital TDS Meter Before Buying

Do not guess what purifier you need. Measure your kitchen tap water with a ₹250 TDS meter. If your municipal supply already reads 80–180 ppm, installing a pure RO membrane strips water down to tasteless 15–20 ppm and wastes 2–3 liters of water for every 1 liter purified—a Gravity or Electric UV+UF purifier is healthier and saves thousands in annual membrane replacements.

2. Watch Out for the Annual Filter & AMC Cost Trap

  • A ₹7,000 budget purifier with locked proprietary RFID cartridges that force ₹5,500/year brand-only service kits costs more over 4 years than a ₹14,000 standard purifier with ₹1,800 sediment/carbon replacements.
  • Look for high-recovery RO membranes (claiming 45–60% water recovery instead of 25%) and route reject water into a bucket for mopping or washing floors.

3. Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What is the ideal drinking water TDS level according to BIS/WHO?
Water between 50 ppm and 150 ppm tastes crisp and retains balanced minerals. Anything up to 300 ppm from clean municipal sources is considered safe by BIS standards.
❓ Do 'Copper' or 'Alkaline' RO cartridges provide real benefits?
They marginally raise pH from slightly acidic post-RO (~6.5) back toward ~7.5–8.0 and add trace minerals, improving taste—just make sure they are replaced on schedule so they don't harbor biofilm.
❓ How often do RO sediment and carbon filters need changing in India?
Every 6–12 months depending on mud/chlorine load in your water; the core RO membrane itself typically lasts 18–24 months.
❓ Does a UV+UF purifier work without electricity?
UF (hollow-fiber ultrafiltration) works purely on gravity/water pressure and removes bacteria/cysts, while the UV lamp requires electricity to neutralize viruses.
