1. Comfort & Indian Humidity: The Spec Nobody Lists

Over-ear headphones using cheap PU faux-leather earpads trap heat within 20 minutes in non-AC Indian rooms. Look for breathable protein-leather cups with large internal oval cutouts (at least 60mm tall) so your earlobes sit inside the cushion instead of getting pinched against the driver grille.

2. Why Multipoint + Wired AUX Matter More Than 50-Hour Claims

If you work on a laptop and take calls on a phone, Dual-Device Multipoint Bluetooth (found on Soundcore Q20i, JBL Tune 770NC, Sony WH-CH720N during sales) lets you pause a YouTube tutorial on your PC and answer a WhatsApp call on your phone seamlessly without re-pairing.

3. Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Soundcore Q20i vs JBL Tune 770NC vs Sony WH-CH520 under ₹5,000?
Soundcore Q20i and JBL Tune 770NC (in sales) are true over-ear ANC models; Sony WH-CH520 is an on-ear model without ANC—choose over-ear if you wear headphones 3+ hours daily.
❓ Can I use Bluetooth ANC headphones for competitive PC gaming via Bluetooth?
No—standard Bluetooth adds 120–220ms lag and drops audio quality when the mic activates. Use the included 3.5mm AUX cable on PC for zero latency.
❓ How long do PU leather earpads last in India?
Usually 12–18 months of daily use before peeling. Pick popular models (Soundcore, JBL, Sony) where ₹400–₹700 replacement earpads are easily sold on Amazon.
❓ Does USB-C audio work on budget wireless headphones?
Only on select models—most ₹3k–₹5k wireless headphones use USB-C strictly for charging and rely on 3.5mm AUX for wired playback.
