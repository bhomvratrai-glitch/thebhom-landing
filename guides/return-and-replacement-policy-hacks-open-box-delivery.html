1. The Golden Rule of Open Box Delivery (OBD): Never Give OTP Early

Some delivery agents ask for the 4-digit OTP first 'so the app lets me open the box.' Refuse politely. Once you share the OTP, the order marks as 'Accepted in good condition' and seller liability ends for physical damage or missing items.

  • Check that the outer shipping label matches your order ID and shows zero retaping.
  • Inspect the laptop/phone screen under bright light for hairline cracks, dead pixels, or missing charger bricks.
  • Verify the IMEI / Serial Number on the phone (`*#06#`) matches the box and tax invoice before sharing the OTP.

2. How to Film a Bulletproof Unboxing Video for Amazon/Flipkart

For non-OBD high-value orders (SSDs, GPUs, monitors, watches), record one continuous, unedited video in good light: first show all 6 sides of the sealed parcel and shipping label clearly, then cut the tape on camera, pull out the item, and verify serial/power-on without ever letting the box leave the frame.

3. Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What if the delivery agent refuses to wait while I inspect an OBD package?
Show them the Flipkart/Amazon OBD policy screen in your order page; if they still refuse to open or let you check, decline delivery and let the OTP expire so your refund processes automatically.
❓ What does '7 Days Service Center Replacement' mean on Amazon/Flipkart?
It means if the phone or laptop has a hardware defect (not physical damage), the delivery boy won't pick it up—you must visit the brand's authorized service center (Apple/Samsung/HP/Lenovo) to get a DOA certificate, after which the marketplace replaces or refunds it.
❓ What if my return pickup keeps getting cancelled or rejected?
Escalate via the platform's Grievance Officer email listed on their website, and file a free complaint on the Government National Consumer Helpline (consumerhelpline.gov.in or 1915 / WhatsApp 8800001915).
❓ Can I return an item just because I didn't like the color or performance on electronics?
No—most electronics in India are now Replacement-only for defects, not 'change of mind' returns, so finalize specs before ordering.
