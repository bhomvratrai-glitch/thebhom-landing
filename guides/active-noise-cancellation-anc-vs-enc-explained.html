1. Why ₹1,299 'ANC' Buds Often Confuse Indian Shoppers

Brands frequently write 'Noise Cancellation' in giant bold text on Flipkart and Amazon listings when the earbuds only have ENC for phone calls—not ANC for blocking traffic while listening to music. True ANC requires inward/outward microphones plus a DSP chip generating inverse anti-phase sound waves inside your ear canal.

2. Feedforward vs Hybrid ANC: What the dB Rating Means

Budget ANC earbuds use single outward (feedforward) mics cancelling ~25–30dB of narrow low bass. Hybrid ANC adds an inward feedback mic inside the ear nozzle, adapting to ear shape and cancelling deeper 38–48dB across wider frequencies—plus minimizing annoying wind roar during bike commutes or ceiling-fan breezes.

3. Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Can ANC completely block someone talking next to me?
No. ANC neutralizes steady low frequencies (50Hz–800Hz engines/fans). Sudden human speech depends mostly on tight passive ear-tip seal (or over-ear clamping).
❓ Why do my ears feel 'pressure' when ANC turns on?
Cheap ANC creates an acoustic 'cabin pressure' sensation. Higher-quality buds and Transparency mode reduce this feeling; switching to medium silicone tips also helps.
❓ Does ANC drain earbud battery significantly?
Yes, expect roughly 20–30% shorter playback per charge with ANC turned on (e.g., 7 hours off vs 5 hours on).
❓ What makes call quality good in Indian traffic?
Look for at least 4–6 microphones (2–3 per bud) with dedicated wind-mesh shielding and bone-conduction or AI ENC beamforming.
